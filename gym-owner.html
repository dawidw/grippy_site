Built for how gyms actually run

🧱

Walls, not just a list

Model your gym the way it's actually laid out — every physical wall gets its own page, incline, cover photo, and a Current/History split.

📦

Sets & publishing

Group boulders into sets, work on them unpublished while you set, and flip them live for climbers the moment you're done.

🔔

Reset heads-up

Tell climbers when a wall's next reset is coming, so a stripped project isn't a surprise — and they've got a reason to come back and see what's new.

🧭

Gym profile

Logo, cover photo, opening hours, address, and a map pin — everything a climber needs before they walk through the door.

🛠️

Setter tools

AI-assisted hold detection, grading in V-scale/Font/gym grades, tags, and beta notes — the same fast flow climbers already use for their own projects.

👥

Scoped admin access

Give your setters gym-admin access limited to your gym only — they manage your walls and boulders, nothing outside it.
