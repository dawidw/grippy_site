How it works

From wall to logbook in four taps

01

Snap the wall

Shoot the wall or pick a photo you already have. No calibration, no special gear — just point and shoot.

02

AI finds every hold

Grippy's built-in hold detector outlines every hold on the photo automatically. Missed one? Fix it with a tap.

03

Track every problem

Grade in V-scale, Font, or gym grades. Tag by style, add beta per hold, and filter to find what you're working on.

04

Log every session

Group boulders by session — solo or with your climbing squad — and see your month at a glance, streak included.

Built for your crew

Never forget a custom route from your section again.

Someone in your climbing section sets a brutal new problem mid-session? Snap it, mark the holds, drop a note on the crux — it's saved to that exact session. Come back next week and it's all still there: grade, tags, and the beta you'd have otherwise forgotten by Tuesday.
